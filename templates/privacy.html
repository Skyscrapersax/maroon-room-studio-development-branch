IN THIS PROTOCOL01 / Sharing02 / Cookies03 / Retention
01

What gets shared?

The private studio desk can read your request. Your private status link shows the project name, service, requested time and decision. Anyone with that link can see those details, so keep it private. Email and notes are not included on the status page or in calendar downloads.

02

Cookies and messages

The app uses an essential session cookie for form protection and studio sign-in. It includes no advertising trackers or third-party images. This pilot does not send email, SMS or calendar invitations. Calendar files are downloaded only when you choose.

03

Retention and corrections

Requests, decisions and cancellation history remain in the studio’s local database until the operator removes them. There is no automatic deletion schedule. Short-lived, keyed network identifiers limit repeated form submissions; expired counters are cleared on later submissions. Ask the studio operator to correct or remove your information{% if contact_email %} at {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}.
